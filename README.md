# The Sample Room

**Pros, cons & scores** opens a private review for each of the four options. Five criteria—portability, intuitiveness, sample visibility, cost efficiency, and compact storage—use editable five-level ratings (1 Poor through 5 Excellent), starting unrated. Pros, cons, and each score save immediately to separate browser-local keys, survive window closure, and remain consistent between the shelf and close-up pages. Notes are not uploaded or shared; use the same browser profile and site address. Clearing site data or ending a private browsing session can remove them. Storage failures are shown explicitly.

All four cases use the [UT Southwestern primary palette](https://brand.utswmed.org/color-palette-and-typography/): blue `#004c97`, navy `#00355d`, cyan `#009ee2`, and gray `#636466`, with neutral lining tints. The **Finish** control offers cloth, tinted wood, and plastic with distinct weave, grain, and surface response. On the shelf, choose one model or all four; close-ups customize that model. Finish and color selections are saved locally per model and follow navigation. Reset restores the coordinated defaults: navy cloth binder, blue wood case, blue cloth wave drawer, and gray plastic pocket box. Product swatches, sample thicknesses, case dimensions, hardware, and animations are preserved.

The Blender and GLB downloads include the coordinated default finishes. Browser finish selections customize the interactive preview. After rebuilding geometry, run `build-case-finishes.py -- REPO` in Blender to apply the default palette to all four downloads; append a model ID to update only that model. The script generates the six local finish textures, packs tinted textures into each Blender file, and exports the animated GLBs. Follow with `compact-glb.cjs` for the rebuilt models.

The homepage displays the **Binder**, **Slanted case**, and **Pocket box** on a compact two-tier open rack with 18 mm shelves and slim 14 mm supports. The **Wave drawer** sits directly on the floor in a portable fabric carry case alongside, with clear pull-out space. A simple scale bar accompanies the close-up shelf view. All four retain the same physical scale in metres; models are not individually resized to fill compartments. Click a model or its label to zoom toward it and open its interactive study. Each study has a back-to-shelf button and four persistent model switches. Archive opens the previous cabinet at `archive.html`, including its local material editing tools and saved collections.

Each current study has eighteen square material cuts at their original thickness. Samples detach, flip to their information cards, and return to their saved mounting positions. The binder retains four corner snaps. The slanted case has a 2.75-inch-deep tray with each reel aligned parallel to its 25° carrier slot beneath the board. Every sample is connected to a modeled retractable reel, with continuous cable extension during pickup and return. Inspection frames the sample, cable, and reel together, revealing the recessed reel at its actual attachment position. The wave drawer includes the phased camshaft, roller followers, return springs, and cutaway controls.

Reel reference: [RUIWOR RW5101](https://www.ruiwor.com/rw5101-round-mini-anti-lost-recoiler-eyelet-terminal-with-ring-terminal-inner-hole-3mm-4mm-5mm-for-option-product/), Ø25.2 × 8.3 mm housing (27.2 mm including the outlet), 0.6 mm cable, 550 mm travel. This was the smallest suitable housing with published dimensions found in the comparison. See `tether-source.json`. Housing surface details, recesses and adapter brackets are design proposals, not manufacturer CAD; the return force takes up cable slack rather than hoisting the sample.

The compact wave drawer has a single padded blue fabric case modeled at exactly 5 × 13 × 7 inches (width × depth × body height), with the former upper drawer removed. A raised luggage handle with a padded grip and reinforced sewn anchors sits on top. Sewn navy binding, opaque cloth sides, and soft webbing pulls complete the case. Its blue `#004c97` and cyan `#009ee2` come from the [UT Southwestern color palette](https://brand.utswmed.org/color-palette-and-typography/). Packed weave and thread-normal textures are retained in the Blender and GLB downloads. Sample pickup lifts vertically above the rim before moving forward and flipping; returning follows the same path in reverse, with correct drawer occlusion. The wider tray, rails, guide anchors, and reel mounts fit the 5-inch body. Samples, retractors, and cam motion retain their original sizes; total height including the handle is 8.03 inches. Build just this model with `build-mini-studies.py -- REPO wave`, then `build-tethers.py -- REPO wave` and `compact-glb.cjs wave-model.glb`.

Blender models, animated GLBs, and source packages are available through each study's information button. `build-tethers.py` adds hardware to the existing study models; the optional arguments after the checkout path select `binder`, `case`, `wave`, or `pockets`. The viewer uses the exported outlet and eyelet nodes to animate the cables. Dimensions and animations are retained by `compact-glb.cjs`, which only shares duplicate binary buffers.

Outer-edge dimension lines measure the actual container geometry in inches on the shelf and in each close-up. The wave case has exact whole-inch body dimensions; other studies retain their fractional dimensions. They follow orbit and zoom, hide during sample inspection, and retain the container dimensions as samples or lids move. The binder labels its open width, depth and spine; cases label their closed height; the fabric carry case measures its body only, excluding its raised handle.

## Local materials

Use the folder-plus icon at the upper right to edit a cabinet. Choose the cabinet and set its sample count: 1–24 acrylic holders, 1–30 binder swatches (up to five pages), 1–10 slanted panels, or 1–12 foam blocks. Counts are independent, and reducing a count preserves hidden texture assignments if you increase it again.

New samples start with a neutral textured surface. Click any sample in the 3D view or cabinet grid, then click its preview or **Replace image** to choose a local image. This replaces only that slot; filenames become sample names. The same dialog can reuse an imported image or restore the default texture. Canceling a picker or choosing an unreadable file preserves the previous texture.

**Fill from images**, **Choose folder**, or dropping files fills only the selected cabinet and sets its count to the number of unique supported images, within that cabinet's capacity. Extra images remain in the local library. The library selector and **Fill cabinet** also let you use the included collection, all imported images, or an individual imported folder. Dropping an image while a sample dialog is open replaces that sample instead.

Cabinet counts and assignments are saved in localStorage; imported images are saved in IndexedDB. Existing users who previously selected a local collection keep those materials on the first upgrade. Each cabinet can then be edited independently.

Supported inputs: JPG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, GIF and BMP. Animated images use a still frame. The library holds up to 120 images, at most 20 MB per source image. Images are resized to a maximum 1536-pixel edge and stored in IndexedDB in this browser; 3D surfaces use 512-pixel textures. Duplicate contents are skipped. Invalid or unsupported files are reported without preventing the other files from importing.

The browser opens the native file/folder picker. It cannot read arbitrary local paths or continuously watch a folder. Selecting a folder copies its supported images into the local library. Re-select it to add new images. No imported images or folder contents are sent to GitHub or any server. Browser storage is specific to the site and browser, may be unavailable in restricted/private modes, and is removed when site data is cleared. If saving fails, the current visit can still use the images. Remove an imported image with its × button.

## Running and hosting

Serve this directory over HTTP(S); no build step required. GitHub Pages publishes `main` / repository root. `index.html` is the shelf overview; studies are `binder.html`, `case.html`, and `mini.html?view=wave` or `mini.html?view=pockets`. The previous cabinet starts at `archive.html` and uses `room.js`, `scene-3d.js`, `cabinet-library.js`, `room-3d.css`, `materials.js` and `material-textures.js`. Three.js 0.180.0 is vendored locally in `three.module.min.js` and `three.core.min.js`; see `LICENSE-three.txt`. Runtime assets are self-contained, with no CDN or analytics requests. Older CSS scene files are retained as unused legacy sources.

WebGL2 is required for the 3D scene. When unavailable, a gallery still supports material inspection and importing. Reduced motion removes animated transitions. Included procedural surfaces are illustrative studies; import material photos for your own collection.

# The Sample Room

A minimal axonometric material library in a solid wooden shelf. Four independent pull-outs sit close to the shelf, which remains visible behind the active collection. The taller cases and shelf openings accommodate the samples. Only one collection opens at a time; the others remain still.

The right-hand icons follow the physical shelf order, from top to bottom:

- Foam tray with twelve solid material blocks, with material names etched into their upper faces. Hover or keyboard focus lifts a block and shows its name and finish; click or tap opens inspection.
- Ivory sample box with a raised lid and six slanted bare stone samples. Each has modeled thickness and its own slanted name card beside the panel. Hover lifts the material and card together, with no acrylic enclosure.
- Sample binder in an ivory case. Three modeled rings hold four turnable pages, with six material swatches per page and popper snap mounts behind each swatch. The complete book is rotated 90 degrees across its case. Scroll, swipe, right-side arrows or arrow/Page keys turn pages. Hover lifts a swatch to reveal its mounting.
- The original acrylic collection, open by default. Twenty-four thick frosted acrylic holders enclose solid material cores. Scroll or use up/down, Home/End and Page Up/Down to browse. The whole open drawer moves along its runners while the focused sample stays centered; the cabinet and closed drawers stay still. Material names are etched into the upper corner of each acrylic holder.

Hover details stay hidden until needed. Click or tap a sample for inspection and saving to a browser-local board. Boards export as vector SVG. The same material catalog is shared across the collections.

HTML, CSS 3D transforms, procedural SVG textures and vanilla JavaScript; no build required. Serve this directory with a static server. GitHub Pages publishes main / repository root. Reduced motion removes animated transitions. Material textures are illustrative studies.
